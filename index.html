From the kitchen

My top tips to help get the best results!

A few small details can make a big difference to your next bake.

Measure carefully

Measure ingredients accurately to help achieve consistent, delicious results.

Prepare your tins

Grease and line your cake tins before adding the mixture, so your cake releases more easily.

Let cakes cool

Allow cakes to cool fully before decorating to help keep your icing smooth and lovely.
