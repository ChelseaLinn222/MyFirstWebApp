A Few Baking Tips

Small details can make a big difference when baking.

Measure carefully

Measure your ingredients accurately to help achieve consistent baking results.

Prepare your tins

Grease and line your cake tins before adding the mixture to make removing the cake easier.

Let cakes cool

Allow cakes to cool fully before decorating so that icing is less likely to melt.
